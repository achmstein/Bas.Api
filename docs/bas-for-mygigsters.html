Lodging BAS through a registered agent

Your workers prepare their quarterly BAS inside your app. A registered tax agent checks the figures, then lodges them with the ATO.

What your worker sees

  1. Opens BAS in your app. No second login.
  2. Enters three figures — G1 total sales, 1A GST collected, 1B GST paid.
  3. Taps submit. We confirm it has been sent for review.
  4. Our agent checks it and lodges it.
  5. Status comes back to your app: in review, then lodged.

What sits behind it

Your app→ NightTax→ Xero Practice Manager→ Agent reviews→ ATO

The agent in the middle is the point. Your workers are not lodging alone — a registered agent is accountable for what goes to the ATO, and their lodgement program usually buys more time than the standard due date.

What we need from you

Two things, once. Neither is secret, so email is fine.

  • A short name for your platform — we have assumed mygigsters.
  • A public key file. One command generates it; the matching private half never leaves your servers, so there is no password to send or rotate between us.

What your side builds

About half a day for one backend engineer.

  • One server route that asks us for a short-lived key when a worker opens the screen.
  • The BAS screen itself, web and Flutter.

Worth starting now

Agent authorisation. Every worker must be on our practice's ATO client list before anything can be lodged for them. This has real-world lag, so it belongs in your signup — not at the moment someone taps lodge.

Tax file numbers. We need one per worker; Practice Manager will not create a client without it. That brings the Privacy Act TFN Rule onto both sides, so the data-sharing agreement is best drafted alongside the build.
